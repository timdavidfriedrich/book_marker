Commonplace is a quote keeper for people who read physical books.

For centuries, readers kept a commonplace book: one notebook for every passage
worth remembering, gathered from everything they read. Commonplace is that
notebook, for the books on your shelf.

CAPTURE A PASSAGE
Photograph the page. Commonplace recognises the text on your device, and you
drag to select exactly the passage you want. No retyping, no dictation, no
squinting at a keyboard with a book held open in one hand.

BUILD A LIBRARY
Add a book by scanning its barcode or searching by title. Quotes are filed
under the book they came from, so a passage always keeps its source.

GROUP BY THEME
A quote can belong to more than one book's worth of thinking. Collect passages
across your whole library into themes, and watch an idea assemble itself from
a dozen different authors.

ADD YOUR OWN VOICE
Attach a short voice note to any quote, for the thought you had while reading
it rather than the words on the page.

MADE TO BE READ
Typography chosen for long passages, a warm paper palette, light and dark
themes, and a high-contrast mode. Available in English and German.

Everything stays on your device.
